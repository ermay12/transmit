Transmit is an Android app for language learners. It shows your Google Messages chats with AI translation and a tutor.

## 4. The free week

- With Transmit installed from Google Play, you can try translation and the tutor free for 7 days.
- There's one free week per device. It needs no card, and it doesn't turn into a paid subscription.
- It comes with a limited AI allowance. It ends after 7 days or when that allowance is used up, whichever comes first.
- If you subscribe during the free week, the free week ends and your plan starts.
- To start it, Google Play checks that the app is the real Transmit from Google Play, on a genuine device. Some devices don't pass, and the number of new free weeks each day is limited.
- I may change the free-week offer, or stop offering it, at any time. That doesn't end a free week you've already started, except for abuse (section 12).

## 5. Subscriptions

There are two monthly plans: **Transmit** and **Transmit Plus**. Transmit Plus has twice the AI of Transmit.

- **Google Play bills them.** You buy, pay for and manage plans in Google Play, under the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/). The price is shown in the app and in Google Play before you buy. I never receive your payment details.
- **They renew automatically.** A plan renews each month, and Google Play charges you for each month, until you cancel in Google Play. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. After you cancel, your plan keeps working until the end of the month you've paid for. Uninstalling Transmit doesn't cancel a plan.
- **Upgrading to Transmit Plus** takes effect right away. Google Play charges the full price of Plus, and adds what's left of your Transmit month to Plus as extra days.
- **Switching from Transmit Plus to Transmit** takes effect at your next renewal. Until then, you keep Plus and its AI allowance.
- **Refunds** follow [Google Play's refund policies](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). You can ask Google Play for one, or write to me. When a purchase is refunded, its AI access stops.
- **Payment problems.** If a renewal payment fails, Google Play may put your plan on hold. While it's on hold, the plan's AI stops working until the payment goes through.
- **One device at a time.** A plan works on one device at a time. Using it on another device stops it on the first.
- **Price changes.** If a plan's price changes, Google Play tells you before the new price applies, under its rules.

## 6. The monthly AI allowance

- Each plan includes an AI allowance for each billing period. Translations, Check my translation, Explain, pinyin and romaji all use it.
- The amount shown in the app, such as "around 3,000 translations a month", is an estimate, not a promise. How far the allowance goes depends on how long the messages are and which features you use.
- The allowance resets at the start of each billing period, when your plan renews. Unused allowance doesn't roll over to the next period.
- When it's used up, AI pauses until it resets, unless you upgrade. Texting keeps working, and the original messages always show.

## 8. AI can be wrong

Translations, corrections, explanations, pinyin and romaji come from AI models. They can be wrong or incomplete, miss tone or context, or say something you didn't mean.

- Don't rely on them for anything important, or for medical, legal, financial or safety matters. For those, ask a fluent speaker or a professional.
- Check before you send. Read the version the other person will receive before you send it.
- AI output isn't my advice, and it doesn't speak for me.
